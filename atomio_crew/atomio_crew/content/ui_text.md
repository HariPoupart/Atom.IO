# UI, tutorial and lobby text

## tutorial_run1
- Drag the left joystick to move your atom.
- Your orbiting electron hurts enemies. Touch them with it!
- Orbit hits deal 10. Enemy contact costs you 10 HP. Stay sharp!
- Touch energy orbs to collect them. Spend energy in the lobby.

## tutorial_evolve_prompt
- Evolve now: spend {cost} energy to turn Hydrogen into Helium. More HP, more slots!

## tutorial_run2
- Helium has 2 electron slots. Walk over wild electrons to grab them.
- Hold the right joystick to aim. A drag line shows your shot.
- Release to throw a spare electron for 20 damage!
- You always keep 1 in orbit. Throw only when you hold more than one.

## evolve_success
- You evolved into {element}! +10 HP, new electron slot.
- Fusion complete! You are now {element}.
- {element} unlocked. Keep climbing the table!

## evolve_fail
- Unstable! Energy lost, but fail chance drops to {next_fail_pct}%.
- Atom wobbled. Try again: fail chance is now {next_fail_pct}%.
- Fizzled out! Next fail chance: {next_fail_pct}%.

## not_enough_energy
- Need {cost} energy to evolve. Back to the arena!

## run_over
- HP gone! You banked {energy} energy. Try again!
- Run over. {energy} energy collected. Evolve or retry!
- Core down! {energy} energy kept. Next run goes further.

## win_oganesson
- You are Oganesson, element 118, named for Yuri Oganessian. You climbed the whole periodic table!

## loading_tips
- Orbiting electrons deal 10 damage. Thrown electrons deal 20.
- Enemy contact costs you 10 HP. Every proton gives you 10 HP.
- Evolving costs 50 + 3 x your atomic number in energy.
- Evolving can fail. Each failure lowers the next fail chance by 0.5%.
- Electron slots equal your atomic number. Evolve for more slots and +10 HP.
- You can only throw an electron when you hold more than one.
- Enemies chase when they spot you. Outrun them and they give up.
- Longer runs mean more energy, but enemies get tougher too.
