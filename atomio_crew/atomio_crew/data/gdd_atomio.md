ATOM.IO (GDD by Hari Poupart, 2026-10-18). Single-player Android (landscape) arcade .io game, Unity 6.3 LTS, API 24+, mid-range 2 GB RAM baseline. Built in about 40 hours.
CORE IDEA: Based on the Bohr model. The player starts as Hydrogen (Z=1) and evolves up the periodic table to Oganesson (Z=118). The player's core is a large circle with electrons orbiting it.
MECHANICS: Player HP = 10 x Z (starts at 10, +10 each evolution). Max orbiting electrons = Z. The player always keeps at least 1 orbiting electron and can only release (shoot) electrons when it has more than 1. Orbiting electrons deal 10 damage to enemies and released electrons deal 20. Enemies deal 10 on contact with the player's core. Enemy HP starts at 10 and grows linearly (amount tuned in testing).
LOOP: Lobby -> Arena run (survive, kill enemies, collect energy orbs that spawn naturally or drop from enemies, pick up wild electrons up to the slot cap). Spawn intervals shrink as time in the arena goes on (inversely proportional to time elapsed). At 0 HP the player returns to the lobby with the energy collected and spends it to evolve.
EVOLUTION: Costs 50 + 3 x Z energy. Failure chance = (-1 + 1.04015^(Z-1))%, which is 0% at H and about 99% at Og. A failure loses the energy, and each failure lowers the failure chance by 0.5%. Win = reach Z=118. The arena has no win condition, but energy grows exponentially the longer the player survives. Enemy HP scales with survival time and Z.
CONTROLS/UI: Left virtual joystick = move. Right attack joystick = aim; releasing it fires the newest electron, and a drag vector shows the aim. HUD: top-left element, HP bar, energy, time survived. Top-right exit and settings.
ENEMIES: Spawn randomly far from the player, chase while the player is in sight range, and give up when the player escapes.
TUTORIAL: The first run as H teaches that orbiting electrons damage enemies. After death the player is forced to evolve to He (starter energy = exactly one evolution). The next run teaches throwing spare electrons and picking up wild ones.
RETENTION: Fast early evolutions, then building a large orbiting-electron "defense". Bonus: element groups get distinct abilities and appearances.
CONSTRAINTS: No runtime API or network calls. All AI work happens at editor time, and tier JSON is pre-generated per element before the build. No external dependencies beyond the GDD. Every change is validated at a manual gate and logged in CHANGES.md.
