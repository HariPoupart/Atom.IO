ATOM.IO (GDD by Hari Poupart, 2026-10-18, rescoped 2026-11-12). Single-player Android (landscape) arcade .io game, Unity 6.3 LTS, API 24+, mid-range 2 GB RAM baseline. Built in about 40 hours.
CORE IDEA: Based on the Bohr model. The player starts as Hydrogen (Z=1) and evolves up the periodic table to Neon (Z=10), the first element with two full shells (2 + 8). The player's core is a large circle with electrons orbiting it.
LEVELS: 10 levels, one per element (H, He, Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F, Ne). Each level is its own arena configuration: theme colours, obstacle layout and spawn pressure, plus the player's core look and a small trait for that element.
MECHANICS: Player HP = 10 x Z (starts at 10, +10 each evolution). Max orbiting electrons = Z. The player can release (shoot) any orbiting electron, even the last one, and is defenceless until it collects another (there is no minimum); every run starts with 1 electron. Orbiting electrons deal 10 damage to enemies and released electrons deal 20. Enemies deal 10 on contact with the player's core. Enemy HP starts at 10 and grows linearly (amount tuned in testing).
LOOP: Lobby -> Arena run for the current element (survive, kill enemies, collect energy orbs that spawn naturally or drop from enemies, pick up wild electrons up to the slot cap). Spawn intervals shrink as time in the arena goes on (inversely proportional to time elapsed). All energy collected fills the energy bar. At 0 HP the player returns to the lobby; the bar keeps its progress between runs.
EVOLUTION (energy bar): No cost and no failure chance. The bar's capacity is 50 + 3Z + 5(Z-1)^2 energy, growing faster in later levels (53 at H, 397 at F). Energy orbs are small white dots worth a fixed 2 energy; a killed enemy drops several orbs scattered around its body (more for tougher, later enemies), and if it was killed by a thrown electron the orbs fly into the player from any distance. When the bar is full the player evolves immediately, mid-run, to Z+1: HP refills to the new maximum, one more electron slot, the new element's core look and trait, and the arena switches to the next level. The bar then empties to 0 (overflow is not carried over). Win = reach Neon (Z=10); Neon has no bar and its arena stays playable.
CONTROLS/UI: Left virtual joystick = move. Right attack joystick = aim; releasing it fires the newest electron, and a drag vector shows the aim. HUD: top-left element, HP bar, energy bar (current / capacity), time survived. Top-right exit and settings.
ENEMIES: Spawn randomly far from the player, chase while the player is in sight range, and give up when the player escapes.
TUTORIAL: The first run as H teaches that orbiting electrons damage enemies and that energy orbs fill the bar. H's bar is small, so the first evolution to He comes fast. On first becoming He the game teaches throwing spare electrons and picking up wild ones.
RETENTION: Fast evolutions with the next one always visible on the bar, then building a full-shell orbiting-electron "defense" (2, then 8). Bonus: each element has its own level and look, and element groups get distinct abilities.
CONSTRAINTS: No runtime API or network calls. All AI work happens at editor time, and tier JSON is pre-generated per element before the build. No external dependencies beyond the GDD. Every change is validated at a manual gate and logged in CHANGES.md.
